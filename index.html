1. About My Data

J42 is committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. To learn more about the types of information we collect, how we use it, and your privacy rights, please review our Privacy Policy.

2. How to Download or Delete Your Data

You have full control over your data. You can:

  • Download: Email chalkboystudios@gmail.com with the subject “Data Request” to receive a copy of your personal data in JSON format.
  • Delete: Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account in the app.
  • All deletions are permanent and processed within 30 days.
